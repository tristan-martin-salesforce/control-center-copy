# 04 · Every reasoning engine against the engine guidelines

This is a docs-only audit at base `9030a46`. The rules are in [docs/ENGINES.md](../../ENGINES.md). Line numbers are at `9030a46`. **F** = follows, **P** = partial, **N** = no, **–** = does not apply (the engine spends no tokens, or runs only on request).

The owner asked: "Do the changes and thoughts I introduced for the DCs/time logging/VDP engine change any of the other engines in the app? Should they be introduced in the repo as guidelines moving forward?"

**Answer:** Yes to both. D-103's principles (facts as data, event-driven with a fingerprint first, one dismissal memory, a version on every output and a log of why each rule changed) were written for DCs, but every engine runs into the same problems. Most engines already follow the deeper rules: why, never write, governor, fixtures. Where they fall short it is the same three things: a version stamp with a reason log, a cheap skip before a model call, and thresholds that only a build can change. So D-103 is now [docs/ENGINES.md](../../ENGINES.md), and the carry-overs below are eight backlog items. None of them is a new mechanism per engine.

## Which engines comply, and which are furthest off

- **Comply already, and need nothing from D-103 beyond a CHANGELOG line on their next change:** the resolution check (apart from the job hold, B-123), priority@2 (apart from the coverage input, B-122), and the Assistant's routing and spend governor.
- **Furthest off:** attribution/time and DCs (no version, schedule-driven, no fingerprint; D-103 is the fix and is already planned), then Home and channel briefs (no version, and the hash is checked after the paid call) and inbox reply drafts (a hard-coded model, a missing governor on voice, coverage ignored).
- **Common gaps:** rule 9 (only priority, resolution and the scanner stamp a version, and nothing logged *why* until today) and rule 1 (thresholds held as constants: `STRONG_ASSET_AT`, `CONFIDENCE_FLOOR`, the resolution windows, `READIN_MODEL`).

## What D-103 changes for other engines

1. **Coverage feeds "who matters".** `coverageOf()` and the covered-AE rows are facts that priority's people tier (`inputs.ts:101-102`) and the inbox read-in's triage should read. An ask from a covered AE ranks like one from a known stakeholder. Today only attribution reads coverage.
2. **The event bus and the fingerprint skip are shared.** `engine_events` and "same fingerprint means stop" belong in one library. Priority already does it (`rerank.ts:71-85`) and VDP identity does a version of it (`identity-judge.ts:115`). Briefs, the scanner's non-Slack sources and VDP autopilot's 30-minute poll should use the same one, keeping their schedules as safety nets.
3. **The decision log is shared.** `docs/engines/CHANGELOG.md` and a stamped `rule_version` apply to every engine, not only DCs, and the UI's why fold cites the same version line and event trail on every surface.
4. **One resurface rule.** D-103 (d)'s `resurfaced(baseline, now)` is the pattern for use-case rejects and VDP autopilot failures too. Each engine declares which facts bring an item back.
