# Command Center — UX

The goal is that you spend your day here and only leave through a one-click handoff. The bar is *tenet-level*: if a screen makes you think "where do I start?", it's a bug.

## Principles

1. **Top 3 first.** The landing screen leads with three things to do now. Everything else is collapsed into counts ("12 more today · 4 waiting on others · 9 in triage").
2. **Every row is actionable in place.** Each row offers Reply, Draft, Approve, Log, Snooze, Done or Dismiss without leaving the app. The source link is always present as `↗ Slack` / `↗ Gmail` / `↗ Org62`.
3. **Why, on hover.** Every rank, mapping and "likely done" shows its reasoning and its evidence links.
4. **Suggestions are separate from commitments.** Auto-detected items land in **Triage** until you accept them, or auto-accept when confidence is high. Your **Today** list is never polluted by guesses.
5. **Batch the boring.** Logging hours, VDP gaps and triage are designed as 5-minute *rituals* (Friday log, Monday plan) with keyboard flow: `j/k`, `a` approve, `e` edit, `s` snooze.
6. **Calm notifications.** One morning brief, a pre-meeting nudge 10 min before customer calls, deadline alarms, and urgent DMs from your watch-list. Nothing else.
7. **Keyboard-first plus a command palette.** `⌘K` searches everything and runs commands ("log last meeting to ACME", "draft reply", "what did I promise Dana").
8. **Live, never jarring.** Every page updates on its own as things happen (push, not polling), and nothing moves under you. See "Live updates" below.
9. **Closed for you, never against you.** Finished work may close on its own (Triage ▸ Closed for you, with its reason, the message that settled it and Undo). Both checks follow one rule: an item you reopened, kept ("Still needed?" ▸ Keep) or marked Not done is never closed for you again, and one involving an ask-first person, channel or outside commitment only asks (Looks done). Accepting an item from Triage, snoozing it or setting its account or due date does not stop it closing. When the asker writes to you again in its conversation within the reopen window (Settings ▸ Closing ▸ Reopen on follow-up, on by default), an item closed for you reopens with "Reopened: PERSON wrote again" and Undo.

## The why line and late work (priority@1.1, 2026-11-20)

- **The why under a ranked card is the ranking's why**, in two short parts, on Home's Do now cards and Today's run of day: *why now* first (late, the deadline or meeting, the start-by, the free time before it: "Due Thu 09:30 · start by 15:00 · 7h30m free before it"), then *why it matters* (the top value terms: who, deal, kind: "Brendan (customer) · opp $780k"). A rule every item shares ("customer-facing beats internal") never fills it. The extractor's (or the NBA's) sentence is the detail line under it, on hover and `?`. A card with neither part keeps its sentence.
- **Late work says how late and what to do**, never "start by Mon": a hard deadline reads "1 d late · do it now" for one working day after it passes (the persona's working hours and timezone: a Friday-evening deadline is still in its grace on Monday morning), then "2 d late · late: do now or re-date" and ranks lower (urgency 0.6). A soft or inferred date reads "2 d late · re-date or drop" at once (urgency 0.4). Think ahead's late rows read the same way. A passed reply rule (your manager's 24 h) never makes an item late while its own due date or meeting is still ahead.
- **Likely-done items are not ranked**; they wait for one click in Looks done.
- **Capacity is honest:** work whose start-by is today counts as today's load even when it is due tomorrow, and when today's hands-on minutes exceed the free time the day reads **overbooked**, never "fits".

## Why is this still open? (resolution check, 2026-11-20)

- **Every item's panel has a "Why is this still open?" section** ("Why was this closed?" once it is done), next to "Why is this here?". It loads only when opened and shows data; its one button is **Undo reopen** (below): the last checks of the resolution check in one plain line each ("The cheap model said still yours (30%); left it open."; "Rule D1: your reply after the ask carries what was asked; closed it for you."), what kept it from closing when something did ("not closed because it was reopened twice, so it only asks now"), a link to the line it cited, and what runs the next check ("A new line in its conversation, a revision or share of its file, notes or a Claude session about it, or the nightly sweep").
- **You reopened it.** After you Undo a close for you, the section says "You reopened it" with **Undo reopen**: an undo of your own Undo (`POST /audit/:id/undo`). The item is closed again as the check left it, and it no longer counts as a correction, so the check may close it again (EDGE-CASES CR-01).
- **In shadow mode (the default) it says what the check *would* have done** ("would have closed it for you"), with one quiet line saying the current closers still act. Health shows one line comparing the two for the week: "Resolution check agreed on 94% of 212 closes this week; would have closed 7 more, reopened 2". Its **Change in Settings** link opens Settings ▸ Sending ▸ Closing items for you.
- An item reopened twice after closing for you is never closed for you again; the next "done" only asks (Likely done · Confirm), and the panel says so.

## Ranking: priority@2 (2026-11-21, shadow first)

- **What ranks.** Value × urgency: who it is for, the deal, the kind of work (customer-facing beats internal), its importance, and how soon it has to start. **Start-by is planned backwards** from the deadline through your working hours, minus meetings and the work already committed before it, so tomorrow morning's demo build that has no room tomorrow morning says "start by 14:00" today, and a Friday deliverable with a Thursday onsite starts Wednesday. A customer demo's prep is ready 30 minutes before it; prep for an onsite is ready the working day before.
- **Admin is one quiet row.** Logging time, VDP updates, deal-contribution sweeps and trainings never take a Top 3 slot; they are one "Admin: 4 items · ~25m · next due Fri 16:00" row, today's load only when something in it is due within 48 h, placed in the day's least valuable free gap.
- **Overbooked by N min.** The one wording on Today's meter, Home's meter and the preview. When the day is overbooked the app proposes what to move (lowest value, latest deadline first): Home's briefing draws it; nothing moves until you do.
- **Shadow first.** Settings `priority.engine` (Settings ▸ Budgets ▸ Models and ranking ▸ Ranking, which Health's Ranking line links to) is `shadow` by default: the list you see is still priority@1.1; priority@2 ranks beside it on every re-rank. Today's header has **Preview new ranking**: the session shows priority@2's Top 3 with each row's place in your list and its two-part why. Nothing is saved, and the toggle forgets when the tab closes.
- **Re-ranks itself.** A new item, a due-date or start-by edit, a closure, a calendar sync or a meeting added or moved re-ranks within a minute (debounced); what is on screen stays put unless something becomes "late unless started" (D-73).
- **Learns, with your click.** Reordering, snoozing, "not today", pinning and "do first" are remembered with the ranking's terms. Health's Ranking line shows how often the two engines agree on the Top 3 over 7 days, the biggest difference, and at most one suggestion ("The model keeps ranking bigger deals above the most urgent work; you keep doing the most urgent work first (7 of 9 times).") with **Rank it the way I do** (one bounded weight nudge, undoable) or **Keep as is**.

## Live updates (2026-11-14)

You never refresh. Sitting on any page, you see real things come in: the backend pushes what changed and each view applies it in place. The bar is that nothing ever jumps under your cursor or your eyes.

**Push, not polling.** One `EventSource` per tab (`GET /api/v1/events`, `src/client/live.ts`) carries `{topic, ids, reason}`. Every database change publishes: the audit trigger for audited tables, `0026_live_notify` for the bookkeeping tables (jobs, scan runs, tool runs, app events, worker instances, the VDP / Forecast / Org62 / landing mirrors), the worker's own events for its catch-up progress, and the web app notices a worker that stopped beating. One table → topic map (`src/server/events/topics.ts`) serves both buses.
- A burst refetches once: a topic's events within 120 ms make one refetch, and chatty topics (jobs, scans, health, tool runs) refetch at most every 1.5 s, always with a last one after the burst.
- A view built from other topics follows them (`useLiveFollow`: Home follows items, outbox, health, deals…; a VDP push, a backfill and a Forecast sync follow `jobs` while they run).
- The clock is an event too: Home and Today refetch right after a meeting start or end, or a due time, they show (`useRefetchAtBoundaries`).
- Polling is only a safety net where push can't see a change: Today, Health and Home every 5 minutes (worker heartbeat, "in 10 min" labels). Nothing polls every few seconds.
- When the stream drops (sleep, a deploy, a restart), the sidebar's status line says "Reconnecting…" in grey after a few seconds, never at once. When it's back, every view refetches once, so nothing is stale after a wake. A silent connection (no frame for 45 s, or a timer gap that means the Mac slept) is reopened.

**One pattern, every list** (`src/components/lists/live-list.ts`, `useLiveList`; the merge is pure, `live-merge.ts`):
- **Stable order.** Rows already on screen never move because of a background refresh. A re-sort by the server waits until you change tab, filter or page. Ranked lists (Today's run of the day, Agents' groups) keep their own explicit apply: "Re-rank ready · N" and "N changed · Re-group".
- **Arrivals enter at their natural place** (before the next row the server puts after them; the top of a newest-first list), with a short tint that settles in about 3 s: blue for something new, violet for what the AI made (drafts, Triage and Needs-you suggestions). Never a flash, never a toast.
- **The pill.** When more than three arrive at once, or you are scrolled down or interacting (the pointer moving over the list, a button pressed, typing in a field in it), arrivals wait behind a calm "N new · Show" pill at the top of the list. A few that waited only for your pointer to rest slide in on their own; Show places them all and brings the first into view. A list that can hold always draws the pill (tests/live/held-pill.test.ts: a held row with no pill is a row you cannot see). Lists of what waits on you (To log ▸ Ready to log, Fix or approve) never hold a burst: rows you approve or that come in for you show at once, and only typing in or scrolling the lane holds them, behind the pill; **Log N · h** always counts every row it takes, held or not.
- **Paged lists keep a window.** A long list (Work ▸ Closed) shows its first 50 and "Show 50 more · N of M shown"; Show more appends the next page under what is on screen and from then on a refresh reads the whole window, so the merge rules hold over every page loaded. Counts are always the server's, never the rows loaded.
- **The scan ledger pages too.** Health ▸ Ledger shows the newest 100 runs live and **Show 100 more · N of M runs loaded** under them; Show more reads the next page by the cursor (GET /scans `cursor` → `nextCursor`), down to the first run ever (caps audit F-2).
- **Departures fade in place.** A row done, sent or applied elsewhere fades where it is, then collapses (under 2 s). A row you removed yourself leaves at once (the app tells yours from elsewhere: `src/client/intent.ts`). Undo puts a row back where it was.
- **Selection, focus and scroll survive.** The selected row stays selected when rows arrive above it; a section's cap grows to keep it in view. If it leaves, the selection moves to its neighbour (j/k keep their row), never back to the top.
- **The open panel stays on its item.** Fresh data updates its fields in place. If the item leaves its list because of something elsewhere, the panel does not close or jump: it says so ("Sent or applied elsewhere", "Handled elsewhere", "Changed elsewhere") with Next and Close, and its row stays, dimmed, until you move on. When you acted on it here, the panel moves on to the next one as before.
- **A field you are editing keeps your text.** If it changed elsewhere meanwhile, the field says "Changed elsewhere: <their value> · Keep mine · Take theirs" (detail fields, Activity hours). Untouched fields update in place. A draft changed elsewhere lists every word span only in theirs and only in yours (a long list scrolls) before Keep yours / Take theirs (caps audit F-13).
- **No loading flashes.** A background refresh never shows a skeleton, an empty state or an error over data you can see (a failed refresh keeps the last data; the lists that can say "Couldn't refresh"). Counts and badges update in place with a small tick.
- **Toasts only for what you did** (a send you approved verified, a run you started finished) or must act on (a new build to reload). Background events never toast.

Per page: **Home** (cards and rows arrive marked, Do now keeps three while one fades out), **Today** (arrivals join below with "N new ↓", "Re-rank ready" applies a new order), **Triage**, **Outbox** (a new draft lands above the one you selected), **Work**, **Deals** (needs and accounts), **Briefs**, **Agents** (runs live; the layout holds while you hover or have one open), **Activity** (To log's ready rows, batches and Needs you's suggestions, the Review page, VDP push progress), **Health** (connectors keep their place; a state change repaints the row) and the sidebar counts all follow the pattern. The mock scenario clock (`CC_SCENARIO=1`) plays arrivals in the first minute: a draft, a Triage suggestion, a Needs-you row, an agent run, then a draft sent elsewhere and a canvas applied in Slack.

## Needs you: one queue (phase 3, 2026-11-21)

Everything that waits on you is one server-built queue, `GET /needs` ([D-86](decisions/0086-needs-you-queue-and-nav.md); `src/engines/needs`), one row per fact: a guess to confirm, a draft to review, unlogged time (one row, however many activity rows), a VDP that needs you, a DC to claim, a question to answer, a spend ask, a Looks done or Still needed?, a setup step, a use case to add, an item that was reopened. Each row has a kind (its badge), one primary verb and its keys (`a` the primary, `n` / `x` the dismiss, `s` Later, 1–9 an answer), a why line, the source link (`o`), the account, an estimate and a group (Today · This week · Deals · Admin). Order: urgency (priority@2's term for a dated work item, else the kind's), then oldest first; never capped.

- **Where it shows.** Home's "Needs you" block (its top rows, under Do now; Looks done is its Confirm kind now), and Activity's "Needs you here" slice (time to log, DCs, VDP changes, use cases to add): the first section under To log ▸ Week's tabs (not on To log's lanes, whose content is the slice: D-102), inside the view's scroll, so the list below never starts under the fold. The sidebar's one Needs-you count sits on Home and is the queue's total, so every surface says the same number.
- **Deciding.** A verb runs the route the page already calls, through `POST /needs/:id/decide`: the row leaves at once, the toast offers Undo, and a failure puts it back with the reason. Navigation verbs go to their page: **Review** a draft in the Outbox (approving keeps its preview, confirm and undo window), **Review** a VDP on Activity ▸ VDP, **How to fix** a setup step on Health ▸ Setup. **Later** on a row without its own snooze is the existing Snooze decision until the next working morning.
- **Every answer is reachable.** A question row shows its first three answers as buttons and the rest in a **More… (N)** menu beside them (keys 1–9 pick the first nine); no choice is dropped (caps audit F-13).
- **Review N** opens drain mode (ReviewQueue) over the rows decided here: `a` the primary verb, `s` Later, `x` the dismiss, `z` undo the batch.
- A kind the persona lacks never shows (no DC rows for an AE); a source that cannot be read leaves its rows out and says "Some sources could not be read". Triage, the Outbox and Activity's tabs keep their own lists and tools while the pages move onto the queue.

## Home: the briefing, and Plan (phase 3, 2026-11-21)

Home is the one page that answers "what do I do now" ([01 R2/R3](reviews/2026-11-20-holistic/01-ui-audit.md), [07 R6](reviews/2026-11-20-holistic/07-prioritization.md), H-10). Top to bottom (`src/components/home`, data from `GET /home`, `src/engines/home/briefing.ts`):

- **The sentence** for the day, the date line (Home | Plan · Today | the next working day) and the day ribbon. **Overbooked → what to move:** when the day meter is over, one line under the sentence says "Overbooked by 45 min. Move “Umbrella deck” to Monday to fit." with **Move it**: the existing snooze to the next working day's start (audited; the toast's Undo brings it back). It is priority@2's makeRoom: lowest value, latest deadline first, never pinned work or a hard deadline today. When nothing can move, the line says so and has no button.
- **Do now: three cards** from the shown ranking (Settings `priority.engine`: priority@1.1 for `v1` and `shadow`, priority@2 for `v2`), each with the two-part why and its own verbs. When drafts are ready, **the third slot is the drafts card** ("Approve 2 drafts" → Review, the Outbox). A card closed for you while Home is open stays in its slot with **✓ Closed for you**, the reason and **Undo** (the reopen Triage ▸ Closed for you runs) until you leave Home.
- **Respond:** one line of chips for the replies the app drafted or can draft: "Reply to Dana · Send", "Post in #team · Send", "Reply to Marcus · Retry" (a failed send), "Answer Lee · Draft" (no draft yet), "… · Blocked" (it waits on someone). A draft chip opens its Outbox row (the Outbox's preview, confirm and undo window); a reply chip opens the item. Nothing sends from Home. The line says what it hides: "+N more" when chips don't fit (the click shows every chip the briefing sent, at most five), then "+N more on Work" (Work ▸ Asked of me, where every reply is). "Review N in the Outbox →" sits at the end. It replaces the Reply & approve column.
- **Needs you:** the one queue, grouped (Today · This week · Deals · Admin) with each group's count in the header; Today's first three rows open, the other groups folded one click away; **Review N** opens drain mode. The header's number is the queue's total, the sidebar's.
- **Coming builds:** agent runs in flight (a demo-org build, a dataset), one row each with its ETA ("ETA 10:40" from its progress, "Needs your answer", "Queued · about 1h 30m") and **Open in Claude** (the session's link, on your click; **Review prompt** first when the session is not started yet, below). Nothing when none run.
- **Admin: one quiet row** ("Admin: 4 items · ~25m · next due Fri 16:00") that expands to its items. With priority@2 shown it is priority@2's batch (promoted to "part of today" when one is due within 48 h, late, or must start today) and admin never takes a Do-now slot; with priority@1.1 it lists the persona's admin items (log time, VDP, deal contributions, trainings) without the batch math.
- **Think ahead · Log & fix · Chase** (and Initiatives at ≥ 2,000 px), then the footer.
- **Empty day:** "Nothing needs you right now. Watching Slack, Gmail and Calendar; anything new lands here."

**Home ▸ Plan** (`/?view=plan`, key `p` both ways) is Today folded into Home: the Today page's own tree (the meter, the day column, the run of the day, the agent's plan tab), so `/today` and Plan never differ. After hours the meter counts the next working day (MIN-22). The topbar still reads Home.

**Narrow Home** (R11): under about 900 px of content (the Details panel open at 1440, or a small window) Home is one reading column: the Do now head wraps, the first card takes the row and the other two share the next, the verb columns stack (Chase too), and the page scrolls instead of clipping. It follows the width only, so a background refresh never switches the layout under you.

Above the fold at 1440×900 and 1920×1000: the date line, the sentence, the ribbon, the three cards, Respond and the Needs-you header.

## Component kit (2026-11-20)

New UI is built from a small kit in `src/components/kit` on top of the `@/ui` primitives. `/dev/kit` (dev only) is its spec: every component in every state, with sample-data placeholders. Pages move onto it one at a time.

- **ActionRow**: the one row. Slots: lead (a when, a step number), a tick box, title, why line, meta chips (confidence Sure / Likely / Guess, ✦ AI or New, the source link "Open in Slack"), one primary verb (quiet until the row is selected), overflow verbs (three icons on hover, the rest under ⋯), custom trailing controls (pickers, mapping choices) and an inline expansion (Space). States: new, leaving, gone (handled elsewhere while open in the panel), selected, held (parked on something else). Keys on a focused row: Enter details, ⇧Enter page, ⌘↵ the verb, `o` the source, plus the row's own single keys. Today's WorkRow, TriageRow, OutboxRow, NeedRow, ReadyRow and VdpItemRow are configurations of it (the map is in `ActionRow.tsx`).
- **QueueSection**: a verb-named section ("Reply 4"), with Select all, See all and a cap that grows to keep the selected row in view. It applies fresh data through the live-list pattern above (stable order, the "N new · Show" pill, fades), so a page gets live updates by using it.
- **PageHeader**: the page title from the route table, a sentence, tabs and the right-side actions. The title never changes with the tab.
- **DetailPanel**: the Details panel frame. Its body is a list of sections registered per entity kind (`registerDetailSections`); its footer has the two item menus. **Done ▾**: done by me · done by someone else · not mine · not needed. **Later ▾**: tomorrow · next week · pick a date · not now (the next working day). Keys: `d` done by me, `⇧D` opens Done ▾, `l` opens Later ▾. Each choice is one of the existing item actions (done, resolve, snooze, not-now); the API does not change.
- **LanePage**: the frame for dense pages (Activity first, then VDP, Inbox, Work): N lanes, each a labelled `section` (`#lane-<id>`) with a sticky header line (title, one count, one primary button, an optional red ▲ failure mark) and a body. Two columns when `#cc-main[data-panel="closed"]` and the `main` container is at least 1,100 px (`LANE_TWO_COLUMN_MIN`): the first lane left, the rest stacked right (`span` overrides). Otherwise one column: a row of lane pills (buttons, `[Name N]`) that open and jump to a lane, and a lane with work is open, its rows showing; only lanes with no work (`collapsed`) start at their header line, and the user's own toggle wins. Chrome drops a size container's rows when the panel toggle flips the layout, so each switch takes every lane body out of layout and puts it back (R3-1). The columns are a container query plus the attribute (no flash on load); `useLaneColumns` mirrors the rule in JS and the frame carries `data-columns="1|2"`. Spec: `/dev/kit/lanes`. To see the one-column state in a screenshot: `npm run shot -- /activity --open-panel` (presses `]`).
- **ReviewQueue**: the drain mode for any queue of decisions (drafts, Needs you, Triage, DC claims). Full height, one card at a time, "3 of 11" and a progress bar. Keys: `a` approve, `e` edit, `s` snooze, `x` dismiss, `j` / `k` move, `z` undo, Esc exit. Same verbs in a row make one batch, and the toast ("Approved 6 · Undo") undoes only that batch. An approve that fails comes back as a Retry card, never a toast. Rows that arrive while you drain join the end; a row handled elsewhere says so and lets you move on. At the end: "That's everything", what happened, and when the next check runs. Approving runs the queue's own approve path, so *Nothing leaves without approval* holds: an Outbox approve keeps its preview, confirm and undo window.

Rules: new rows use ActionRow, never a new `*Row` component. New component code uses Tailwind and the tokens in `app/globals.css` only: lint warns on a new `*.module.css` import under `src/components` (the files that had one are allowlisted in `eslint.config.mjs`), and on decision ids (`D-63`) or Salesforce API names (`Use_Case__c`) in JSX text; `src/components/kit/__tests__/internal-ids.test.ts` fails on a new one (the existing ones are listed in `src/components/kit/lint/internal-ids-grandfathered.json`, to shrink).

## Keyboard and screen reader (2026-11-21)

Everything on every page works with a keyboard and reads right with a screen reader; `tests/a11y/axe-pages.ts` (axe-core, WCAG 2.1 A and AA plus the landmark and heading rules) checks every page in both themes and fails on a violation its baseline does not list ([30-a11y](reviews/2026-11-20-holistic/30-a11y.md)). The rules it and the kit now hold:

- **Skip link first.** The first tab stop on every framed page is **Skip to content** (kit `SkipLink`): Enter moves focus to the page's first row or control (so j/k and the row keys work at once), else to `#cc-main` (`tabIndex={-1}`). Then Tab follows the screen: sidebar, topbar, the page, the panel.
- **Landmarks.** One `main` (`#cc-main`; onboarding's step column), the sidebar `aside` with its `nav`, the panel `aside`, the topbar `header`. A named section is unique on its page: a wrapper around a section that names itself stays a plain `div`.
- **One h1, levels in order.** The topbar holds the page's h1; nothing inside a page is an h1 (the drain mode's title, an initiative's name and the item page's title are h2; the item page's is screen-reader-only because the title is an editable field). Headings go up one level at a time: a settings `Block` under onboarding's h1 titles with h2 (`BlockHeadingLevel`), the effect pane's cards are h3 under its h2.
- **Grids hold rows, rows hold cells.** A kit `ActionRow` (role `row`) wraps its slots in one `gridcell` that takes no box (`contents`), so its buttons are valid children; every container of rows is a `grid` (or `treegrid` when a row opens, the scan ledger); a group's header is a `row` with a `rowheader`; a list holds only its items (a section heading sits above the list).
- **Names say what you see (WCAG 2.5.3).** A control's accessible name contains its visible label: Search reads "Search or ask…", Assistant "Assistant", Feedback "Feedback: Report a bug / idea", Scan now "Scan now: Slack", the zoom "Fit: whole day shown"; a calendar block starts with what it shows. Icon-only buttons have a label (`IconButton label`). The longer sentence goes in the tooltip (`title`), never instead of the label.
- **Shortcuts.** Key caps on a control are decorative (`KbdHint decorative`, `aria-hidden`) and the control carries `aria-keyshortcuts` (`Button kbd` does it; `ariaKeyShortcuts` turns "mod+k" into "Meta+K"); a hint on its own (the cheat sheet, Settings ▸ Display) is read as its caps. No `aria-label` on a plain `span`.
- **Dialogs, menus, tabs.** A modal (`Overlay`: Dialog, the palette, the cheat sheet, Review prompt) traps Tab, closes on Esc and gives focus back to what opened it; a scroller inside it is a tab stop. A menu (`Menu`) moves with ↑/↓/Home/End and typeahead, closes on Esc, and on Tab from an item (focus goes back to its trigger). Tabs move with ←/→/Home/End; `aria-controls` names a panel only when that panel is on the page. The panel's tablist holds only its tabs; Close sits beside it.
- **Live regions.** Toasts (Undo included) are in a polite live region; the Assistant's thread is a `log` (`aria-live="polite"`, `aria-relevant="additions text"`) and a streaming answer is `aria-busy` until it ends, so it is read once, whole.
- **Forms.** Every field has a label; a settings `Row`'s helper, or the error that replaces it (an `alert`), is the field's `aria-describedby`, with `aria-invalid` on the field.
- **Not colour only.** Status chips carry their word; a dot that means something has a name (the unread dot is `role="img"` "Unread"); a dot that does not is `aria-hidden`.
- **Contrast.** Every token pair in `src/styles/brand.css` meets 4.5:1 for text and 3:1 for UI and focus, light and dark (`npm run contrast`, in the ui tests); axe finds no computed contrast failure on any page. Fix a failing pair at the token, never in a component.
